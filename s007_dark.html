Why is this better?

record_voice_over

“I'd like” sounds significantly more courteous in hospitality settings compared to the demanding nuance of “I want”.

phonelink_ring

Use “an” before words starting with vowel sounds (/ɒ/). Saying “an orange” allows effortless phonetic transition.
