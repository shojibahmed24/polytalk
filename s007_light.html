I want a chicken burger and a orange juice.

south
check_circle Polished Natural English

I'd like a chicken burger and an orange juice, please.

tips_and_updates

Why is this better?

Key conversational rules

1

“I'd like” is more polite and natural than “I want” when ordering food or making requests.

Contraction of “I would like”
2

Use “an” before words starting with a vowel sound. Because orange starts with the vowel sound /ɒ/, “an orange juice” is correct.

A cat, a dog → but an apple, an orange
